Shadow Heist — Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 November 2026 · Developer: Lokman Sahin

Offline gameplay and local storage

Shadow Heist is a single-player game for iPhone and iPad. No account or login is required. Progress, earned currency, unlocked equipment, achievements and preferences are stored locally on your device. The game does not send gameplay data to the developer or to an AI service. It contains no advertising, tracking, analytics or in-app purchase SDK.

The Unity engine accesses local preferences, app files, available storage and elapsed time to run the game. It does not use these accesses to track you. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo-library access.

Support and external websites

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you send. We use them to respond and resolve your request. Email is processed through our email provider, Gmail. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept only as necessary to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You may request its deletion at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Privacy, terms, support and attribution links open in your browser. The legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; its hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the website. We do not embed analytics or advertising on these pages. External websites are governed by their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you delete it. Use iOS/iPadOS Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → Shadow Heist → Delete App to remove the app and its local data. Offload App keeps data. Device backups may retain game data according to your Apple settings; manage backups separately through those settings. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete your local progress.

Changes and contact

This policy describes the current offline version. We will update it if data practices change. Privacy questions: lokman782782@gmail.com.

Yürürlük: 9 Kasım 2026 · Geliştirici: Lokman Sahin
