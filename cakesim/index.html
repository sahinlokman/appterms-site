Ebru marbling cake atelier · Ebru sanatıyla pasta atölyesi

Marble paint on water with a brush, a stylus and a comb, dip the cake into your pattern and decorate it with gold leaf, pearls and cream. Discover the classic ebru patterns for your pattern book, cut the first slice to show the layers inside and earn tulips from every order.
