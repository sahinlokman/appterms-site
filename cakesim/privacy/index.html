Privacy Policy

Updated: October 21, 2026 · CakeSim 1.0.1

About this release

CakeSim does not require a player account. Game progress and cake designs are stored on your device. The optional "No Ads" subscription uses Apple and RevenueCat; ads are provided by Google AdMob. Game Center, iCloud synchronization and notifications are not used.

Developer and contact

CakeSim is provided by independent developer Lokman Şahin. Contact us about privacy or support at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game stores your level, stars, tulips (the in-game currency), unlocked decorations, the patterns in your pattern book, your last sponge flavour, number of cakes made, tutorial state, sound preference and the time the last full-screen ad was shown. It also stores images of the cakes you finish (glaze and photo) and a picture of each pattern in your pattern book so you can see and share them. These files are kept in the app's own data area and are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. Settings → Reset progress deletes them.

Diagnostics

CakeSim does not include its own analytics. Unity Analytics and Unity cloud crash reporting are disabled in this release. The game uses the Unity engine; see the Unity Privacy Policy. If you share crash reports with developers through iOS settings, Apple provides them under its own policy.

Ads, consent and tracking

The free version shows Google AdMob full-screen ads between cakes and offers optional rewarded videos to unlock decorations. Google and its partners may process IP addresses (including approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and other purposes described in the consent screen. Google UMP presents consent choices where required; ads are requested only when it allows. The app then shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; the advertising identifier (IDFA) is available to Google only if you allow tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and revisit available ad choices through Settings → Privacy options. Rejecting consent or tracking does not prevent gameplay. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchase processing

Apple processes payment; we do not receive your card details. RevenueCat processes purchase history, receipts or transaction identifiers, subscription status, an app-generated anonymous user identifier, and technical information needed to validate and restore purchases, prevent fraud and analyze purchase activity. It does not synchronize your game progress. Records may be retained for purchase restoration, fraud prevention and legal obligations. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Sharing and photos

When you tap Share, the game opens the system share sheet with the cake image. Only the destination you choose (for example Messages or Save Image) receives it, under its own terms. If you choose to save the image to Photos, iOS asks for add-only access; the game cannot read your photo library.

Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and chosen attachments. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot, retaining them only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, without advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies added by us. The hosting provider may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local progress remains until you reset it or the app's data is removed; uninstalling may remove it. Device backups can remain according to operating-system settings. Google, RevenueCat and Apple retain service data under their own policies. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests concerning information you sent us, email lokman782782@gmail.com. Rights under applicable privacy law, including KVKK and GDPR where applicable, are not limited by this policy. We do not sell information you send to support.

Children and changes

CakeSim is a general-audience game. It does not ask players for names, email addresses, contacts or precise location. Parents should supervise children using ad-supported features or purchases. If a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Changes to data practices will be reflected here with a revised date. This policy covers CakeSim only, not other games on appterms.site.

Cihazınızda tutulan bilgiler

Seviye, yıldız, lale (oyun içi para birimi), açılan süslemeler, desen defterinizdeki desenler, son seçtiğiniz kek aroması, yapılan pasta sayısı, öğretici durumu, ses tercihi ve son tam ekran reklamın gösterildiği zaman yerel tutulur. Bitirdiğiniz pastaların görselleri (sır ve fotoğraf) ile desen defterinizdeki her desenin resmi de görüntüleyip paylaşabilmeniz için saklanır. Bu dosyalar uygulamanın veri alanındadır ve geliştiricinin işlettiği bir sunucuya yüklenmez. Ayarlar → İlerlemeyi sıfırla bunları siler.

Tanılama

CakeSim kendi analitik aracını içermez. Bu sürümde Unity Analytics ve Unity bulut hata raporlaması kapalıdır. Oyun Unity motorunu kullanır; Unity Gizlilik Politikası geçerlidir. iOS ayarlarından geliştiricilerle hata raporu paylaşmayı seçerseniz bunları Apple kendi politikasına göre iletir.

Reklamlar, izin ve takip

Ücretsiz sürüm pastalar arasında Google AdMob tam ekran reklamları gösterir ve süslemelerin kilidini açmak için isteğe bağlı ödüllü videolar sunar. Google ve iş ortakları reklam sunumu, ölçüm, dolandırıcılığı önleme ve izin ekranında açıklanan diğer amaçlarla IP adresini (yaklaşık konum dahil), cihaz ve reklam kimliklerini, reklam etkileşimlerini, tanılama ve performans verilerini işleyebilir. Google UMP gereken bölgelerde izin seçeneklerini gösterir; reklam yalnızca izin verildiğinde istenir. Ardından Apple'ın Uygulama İzleme Şeffaflığı (ATT) izni sorulur; reklam kimliği (IDFA) yalnızca takibe izin verirseniz Google'a açılır. Bunu iOS Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve Güvenlik → Takip bölümünden değiştirebilir, reklam tercihlerinizi oyunda Ayarlar → Gizlilik seçenekleri üzerinden yeniden açabilirsiniz. İzni veya takibi reddetmek oynanışı engellemez. Google Gizlilik Politikası geçerlidir.

Ödemeyi Apple işler; kart bilgilerinizi almayız.
