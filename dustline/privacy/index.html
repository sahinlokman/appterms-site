DUSTLINE

This policy applies only to DUSTLINE, Android package com.lokman.dustline. Contact the developer at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Local play

Game progress (in-game cash, cars and upgrades, stages and stars, best runs, missions, achievements, purchases owned and the free-gift timer) and settings (language, music, sound effects, vibration, pedal layout, hints) are saved on the device only. There is no account, sign-in, chat or social feature, and the developer runs no game server. The game does not ask for a name, email, date of birth, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included, and Unity's own analytics and crash reporting are switched off. The game plays offline; ads and purchases need an internet connection.

Cloud save and achievements

The achievement list is tracked locally on the device. Cloud save, Google Play Games and Game Center are switched off in this version, so progress is not uploaded anywhere and is not moved to a new device. If any of these is turned on in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Ads and consent

Ads are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Rewarded ads are always optional (for example extra cash, or doubling the free gift); skipping one changes nothing else. Interstitial ads may appear between runs, never in the first few runs and with a minimum gap between them; no banner or app-open ads are used. The “Remove Ads” purchase stops between-run ads, and rewarded ads stay optional. Before any ad is requested, Google's User Messaging Platform checks on every launch whether consent is needed in your region and, if so, asks you first. When Google requires it, ad privacy choices can be reopened from Settings → Privacy options.

On Android, Google may use the device advertising ID (the app declares the AD_ID permission) to show, measure and protect ads, and to personalize them where your consent choices allow; you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may also process the IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are offered: Remove Ads, Income Doubler, the Road Kit bundle (both together), a one-time Starter Crate and cash packs. Google Play Billing handles payment; we never receive card details. The purchase is checked on the device by the Unity In-App Purchasing library, which talks to Google Play; no purchase information is sent to the developer. Google processes your Google account, order and payment details under its own policies. Owned items are remembered on the device and can be restored with Settings → Restore purchases.

Reminders and reviews

The game can send one local notification when the next free gift is ready, but only after you choose “Remind me”; Android then asks for the notification permission. The reminder is scheduled on the device; there is no push server. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time. The game may also show Google Play's in-app review card; Google Play handles it and the developer receives no personal data from it.

Device permissions

On Android the game uses: internet and network state (ads and purchases), vibration (haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings), notifications (the optional gift reminder) and the advertising ID (ads, as described above). It uses no other sensitive permission.

Audience

DUSTLINE is a general-audience game for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and does not ask for an age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child's information was provided to us can contact the email above to request deletion.

Sharing, retention and requests

Data is shared only with Google for advertising, payments and the in-app review card; it is not sold. Local progress stays until you delete the app or its data, or use Reset progress (Settings on the main menu). Operating-system backups, if enabled on your device, are managed by Google separately. Provider records may be retained by those providers for legal, accounting and fraud-prevention purposes. The developer holds no player data; questions can be sent to the email above with the app name. Do not send passwords or full payment details.
