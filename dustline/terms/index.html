The game

DUSTLINE is provided for personal entertainment. In-game cash, cars, upgrades and gifts are in-game items with no real-money value; they cannot be sold, exchanged or cashed out, even when obtained through a purchase.

Purchases

Remove Ads, Income Doubler and the Road Kit bundle are one-time, permanent purchases. The Starter Crate can be bought once per Google account and adds in-game cash once; cash packs can be bought repeatedly. Cash from either is used up when spent and is not restored. There are no subscriptions. The price is shown by Google Play in your local currency before you confirm. Use Settings → Restore purchases to restore permanent purchases with the same Google account. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies; a refunded purchase may be removed from the game.

Ads

Rewarded ads are optional and give their reward only when the ad network confirms it. An ad that is closed early, fails to load or is not available gives no reward and never blocks play. Interstitial ads may appear between runs unless Remove Ads is owned. The free gift is based on the device clock; changing the clock can pause it. Rewards, prices and limits may change in updates.

Availability

We aim to keep the game working but do not guarantee uninterrupted availability of store or ad services. Progress is stored on the device only and can be lost if the app or its data is deleted.
