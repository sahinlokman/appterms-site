Contact

Email lokman782782@gmail.com with the app name, your device and, for purchases, the Google Play order number. Never send passwords or full payment details.

Restore purchases

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google Play account used to buy. Remove Ads, Income Doubler and the Road Kit bundle come back; cash already spent does not. If the store is unreachable, connect to the internet and try again.

Pending or failed payments

If Google Play says the payment is pending (for example, waiting for family approval), the item is delivered once it is approved. A cancelled or failed purchase does not charge you and leaves your progress untouched.

Ads and privacy

Rewarded ads are optional; if none is available, try later. Ad privacy choices are under Settings → Privacy options when Google requires them.

Gift reminders

The free-gift reminder is sent only if you chose “Remind me”. To stop it, turn off notifications for DUSTLINE in your device settings.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device only. Cloud save is not available in this version, so progress is not moved to a new device and is removed when the app or its data is deleted. Reset progress (Settings on the main menu) starts over.

Settings

Music, sound effects, vibration, pedal layout, hints and language can be changed in Settings at any time.
