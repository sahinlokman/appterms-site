RiseSpan: Slope Calculator

Enter a rise, grade or angle plus the Horizontal Run and get grade percent, angle, vertical : horizontal ratio and slope length, with a live triangle. Works offline with metric units and feet, inches and fractions. RiseSpan calculates geometry from the values you enter; it doesn't measure distances or check building codes.
