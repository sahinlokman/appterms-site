RiseSpan Privacy Policy

Updated 21 October 2026. This policy covers the RiseSpan: Slope Calculator app for iPhone and iPad only.

Summary

RiseSpan does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the camera, microphone, location or motion sensors.

Data stored on your device

The values you type, saved calculations, calculation names and notes, projects, input presets, the current draft and your unit and display preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData and UserDefaults. They are not sent to us or to any third party and are not synced through iCloud by RiseSpan. They are included in your device backups according to your own iOS backup settings. Deleting a calculation or project in the app, or deleting the app, removes this local data.

Exports

PDF and CSV exports are created on your device. They leave your device only if you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet (for example Mail, Files or AirDrop); that destination's own privacy terms then apply.

Purchases

The optional one-time RiseSpan Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. RiseSpan uses Apple's StoreKit on your device to check whether Pro is unlocked. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Links

Links to this website or to your email app open only when you tap them. This website may be served by a hosting provider that keeps standard server logs.

Children

RiseSpan is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact
