What RiseSpan does

RiseSpan calculates two-dimensional right-triangle geometry — grade percentage, angle, vertical-to-horizontal ratio, rise and slope length — from values you enter. Horizontal Run means level distance, not the length along a sloped surface.

What RiseSpan does not do

RiseSpan does not measure physical distances or angles, and it does not determine compliance with building, accessibility, plumbing, safety or any other codes or standards. Results depend entirely on the values and units you enter. Check critical work with appropriate measuring tools, the applicable code and a qualified professional. Diagrams may be drawn not to scale and are marked when they are.
