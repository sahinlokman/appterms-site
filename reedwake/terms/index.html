Playing Reedwake

Reedwake is a casual fishing game. In-game coins, pearls, catches, upgrades and rewards are virtual items, have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. Progress is saved on the device; keep your device backups if you want to preserve it.

Free game and optional ads

Core play is free. Interstitial ads may appear at natural breaks after the first three completed runs, subject to a three-run interval and at least 120 seconds between interstitials. Rewarded ads are optional. A run or offline-income bonus adds an amount equal to the relevant base reward once; an equipment reward gives one eligible level. Only one free equipment level is available per completed run, and captain-rank requirements still apply. Ads and optional rewards depend on availability.

Premium Monthly subscription

Premium is a one-month, automatically renewing subscription. While active it removes ads, grants the optional x2 run/offline bonuses instantly and provides the eligible free upgrade without a video. Existing gameplay limits still apply. The U.S. base price is US$2.99 per month; your Apple storefront shows the actual localized price and taxes before purchase. The in-game purchase screen displays Apple’s current price.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm a purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Apple charges renewal according to the price and terms shown at purchase. Manage or cancel it in Apple Account subscriptions. Access remains subject to Apple/RevenueCat’s entitlement status. Use Restore Purchases in the game’s Settings or Premium screen for the Apple Account that purchased it. Refund requests are handled by Apple through Report a Problem.
