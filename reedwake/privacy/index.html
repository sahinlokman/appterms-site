Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

This policy covers the Reedwake fishing game for iPhone, iPad and Android, application ID com.lokmansahin.sazligol, developed by Lokman Şahin.

Game progress on your device

Coins, equipment, fish collection, captain progress, achievements, preferences, offline-income timestamps and pending rewards are saved locally. No game account or developer-operated game server is required. iPhone and iPad backups may retain this local data. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. Deleting the app normally removes its local progress.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple on iOS and Google Play on Android process subscription payments; we do not receive your card details. RevenueCat verifies purchases and restores, manages Premium access and provides subscription analytics. It processes an anonymous app user ID, purchase history and technical app/store information such as platform, app version, language and currency. We do not set a custom user ID tied to your identity or send contact attributes or advertising identifiers to RevenueCat. Apple manages iOS purchases through your Apple Account; Google Play manages Android purchases through your Google Account. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Advertising and consent

The free game uses Google AdMob interstitial and optional rewarded ads. It contains no banner ads. Active Premium members do not receive ads; optional reward benefits are granted without viewing a video. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device/app identifiers, advertising and interaction data, crash logs, performance data and other diagnostics to provide and measure ads and operate its service. We do not request precise location permission.

Google User Messaging Platform checks the consent requirements before ad requests and presents a consent message when required and available. Required privacy options can be reopened in the game’s Settings. On iPhone and iPad, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt controls access to the advertising identifier (IDFA); declining tracking does not block the core game or purchasing. On Android, Reedwake is intended for all ages including children. We do not collect age or date of birth. All Android ad requests use child age treatment, general-audience (G) ad content filtering and the under-age-of-consent setting. Personalized advertising and remarketing are disabled for these requests. The Android advertising-ID and advertising-services permissions are removed from this app. No Apple tracking prompt is shown on Android. Technical identifiers and diagnostics may still be processed for essential service operation, fraud prevention and purchase verification; these settings do not mean that no data is processed. Ad personalization depends on your choices and applicable requirements. See Google’s privacy policy and Google ad controls.

Contact, retention and choices

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to handle your request. Do not send payment-card details. To request help or deletion of identifiable support correspondence, contact lokman782782@gmail.com. Third-party retention and deletion are governed by their policies; anonymous purchase data may require your RevenueCat app user ID or transaction reference to locate. Cancelling a subscription stops renewal and does not automatically erase purchase records. Manage iOS subscriptions in your Apple Account settings and Android subscriptions in Google Play subscriptions.

This website

These pages contain no account, advertising or submission form. GitHub Pages may process technical access logs to host and secure the site.
