Raf Raf: Toy Match 3D

This policy applies only to Raf Raf: Toy Match 3D, package/bundle com.rafraf.game. Contact the developer at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Everything stays on your device

Game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, stickers, events and garden) and settings (language, music, sound effects, vibration, animations) are saved on the device only. There is no account, sign-in, chat or social feature. The game does not ask for a name, date of birth, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. No analytics, advertising, purchase or crash-reporting SDK is included, and the game does not send any personal data to the developer or to third parties. All levels and modes play offline.

No ads and no purchases

This version shows no third-party ads and has no in-app purchases. Buttons labelled “Watch an ad” start a short in-game break with a countdown and then give an in-game reward; no ad network is contacted and no ad identifier is read. Coins, lives and boosters are earned by playing and cannot be bought with real money. If ads or purchases are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Device features

On Android the game may use the vibration permission for light haptic feedback, which can be turned off in Settings. It uses no other sensitive permission.

Audience

Raf Raf is a general-audience game for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and does not ask for an age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Retention and requests

Local progress stays until you delete the app or its data, or use Settings → Reset progress. The developer holds no player data; questions can be sent to the email above.
