Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the RoundCaller iPhone and iPad app handles information. In short: RoundCaller has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Everything you create stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

Your current session (event name, rounds, the shuffled draw order for each round, calls and undo history, target patterns, private host notes, and settings such as voice, speaking speed and auto interval), custom patterns and saved event plans are stored in the app's private storage on your device. A last-known Pro status is also kept on the device so Pro screens open correctly offline. None of this is sent to the developer or any third party.

Voice

Numbers are read aloud with Apple's built-in on-device speech voices. The app does not record audio, does not use the microphone and does not use the camera.

Purchases

RoundCaller Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether Pro is active on your Apple Account; Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy.

External displays

If you connect a TV or projector with AirPlay or a cable, the app shows the public board on that display. Only the current number, call history, target pattern and round information are shown there. Private host notes are never shown.

Exports and sharing

If you use History export (Pro), the app creates a CSV file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. Private host notes are not included in exports.

Children

RoundCaller does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Device backups you have made may still contain it until they are replaced. You can also start a new event in the app; earlier events are archived on the device and removed when the app is deleted.

This website

This page has no accounts, forms, cookies or ads. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process technical access logs to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Changes

If the app's data handling changes, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated first.
