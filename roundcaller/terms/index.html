What RoundCaller is

RoundCaller is a tool for hosts who run 75-ball bingo with physical cards. It draws numbers at random, reads them aloud, keeps the call history, shows target patterns and helps manage rounds. It does not offer wagering, real-money play, prizes, payouts or any casino features.

Checking winners

RoundCaller does not know what is printed on players' cards. It never verifies or declares a winner on its own. The host is responsible for checking claimed bingos against the called numbers and the announced pattern, and for any prizes offered at their event.
