Support

Email lokman782782@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and what happened.

I can't hear the numbers

Turn the volume up and check the speaker connection. Use Settings & Help › Test Voice. RoundCaller uses English voices already on your device; you can download better ones in iOS Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices. If the voice fails during a game, RoundCaller pauses without drawing a new ball. Tap Repeat, or Continue silently.

Auto calling stopped

Auto pauses on purpose when you tap Bingo! Check or Undo, when the app leaves the screen, during phone calls or other audio interruptions, when a speaker or display disconnects, and while the App Store sheet is open. It never resumes or catches up on its own. Tap Resume when you're ready.

How do I check a winner?

Tap Bingo! Check. Use "Was this number called?" and the called-numbers list to compare the player's card with the pattern. RoundCaller can't see cards, so you decide whether it's a bingo.

Showing the board on a TV

Open the Public Board (TV icon) and use Screen Mirroring with AirPlay, or a cable adapter. With RoundCaller Pro, a connected display can show its own board while you keep the controls on your device.

Restore a purchase

Settings & Help › Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account used to buy Pro.
