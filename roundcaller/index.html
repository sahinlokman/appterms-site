RoundCaller: Bingo Caller

A 75-ball bingo caller for hosts who run games with physical cards at home, in classrooms, clubs and small events. RoundCaller calls numbers, keeps the history, shows the target pattern and manages rounds. It is not a gambling app: there are no wagers, prizes or payouts in the app.
