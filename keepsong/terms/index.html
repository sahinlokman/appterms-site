Using Keepsong

Keepsong is a personal story and family connection companion. Use it at the person's own pace and in accordance with applicable recording, privacy, and intellectual property laws.

Connection, with appropriate support

The app does not diagnose, treat, monitor dementia, assess decision-making capacity, or promise to improve memory. It does not replace professional care. Notes and routines appear on screen; they do not send alarms, confirm medication use, monitor activity, or provide emergency assistance.

Recording should be welcome

Only record with the appropriate understanding, consent, and support for everyone whose voice may be captured. Respect a person's choice to decline or stop. The “Recording is welcome” preference is a local setting; it does not establish a caregiver's legal authority, a person's capacity, or legally valid proxy consent. The family is responsible for arranging any permissions or authority required for its circumstances.

Simple mode is a presentation preference. It is not an authentication or caregiver access-control system. A person with access to the device's Settings can return to the family dashboard.

Your content and shared copies

You retain your existing rights in the recordings, photographs, and text you add. Only add or share content that you have the right to use and that respects the privacy of the people involved. The developer does not receive ownership of your personal content merely because you store it in Keepsong.

Sharing sends a copy to the destination you choose. Manage that copy with its recipient or service. Removing content on this device does not recall shared copies or delete device backups.

Local storage and continuity

The current app has no login, developer-operated server, automatic family sharing, or cloud sync. It has no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Keep separate copies of important recordings. Device loss, app deletion, storage damage, or a transfer to another device can affect access; Keepsong does not promise a permanent remote archive or zero possible recording loss.

Features may change through app updates. Current support guidance and limitations are available on the support page.
