Your privacy

This policy describes the local iPhone and iPad version of Keepsong. There is no app account, developer-operated backend, automatic family sharing, or cloud sync.

What stays on your device

  • Names, relationship labels, selected photographs, and whether a familiar person appears on the daily check-in.
  • Voice recordings, their titles, favorites, dates, durations, and the story prompts associated with them.
  • Personal story prompts, a daily note, routines, and preferences such as spoken prompts, simple mode, and recording approval.
  • Technical diagnostic logs and temporary recovery files used to help preserve an interrupted recording.

These items are stored in the app's local storage. Keepsong does not upload them to the developer, an analytics service, an advertising network, or an AI service. It does not use third-party analytics, ads, crash reporting, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Recording and playback work without an internet connection.

Microphone and photographs

Keepsong asks for microphone access when you choose to enable it during setup or in the app's Settings tab. A story records only after you start it. Microphone permission and “Recording is welcome” must both be enabled before a new story can begin. Turn off that preference to prevent new recordings, or revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings. Existing memories remain available.

Photos are chosen with Apple's system photo picker. Keepsong receives the image you select, makes a smaller local copy, and does not request access to browse your entire library. If an image is held in iCloud Photos, Apple's picker may download it according to your Apple account and settings. Keepsong does not request device location or make weather network requests in this version.

Sharing, backups, and security

Share opens the system share sheet only when you choose it. A selected destination receives a copy under that person's or service's own rules. Deleting the original in Keepsong cannot remove a copy that you have shared.

iOS device backups may include app data according to your device settings. Keepsong does not provide its own remote backup, cross-device restoration service, or cloud archive. Export important recordings with Share if you need a separate copy; keep that copy somewhere you trust.

Saved audio, temporary audio, and imported photographs use iOS file protection. Temporary recovery segments are additionally sealed using AES-GCM and a device-local key in Apple's Keychain. This is not end-to-end encryption. Removing that key alone does not erase final playable audio files. Protect access to the device and to your backups.

Retention and your choices

Memories remain in the local library until you choose “Delete this memory” and confirm deletion. Successful deletion removes the local audio and its catalog entry. Recovery files are removed after a completed recording has been safely added to the library. If an error prevents saving or deletion, the app keeps recoverable data and shows guidance.

You can change names, selected photos, prompts, notes, and routines in the family dashboard. Hiding a person or disabling a prompt changes what is shown; it does not erase that information. The daily note stays until you change it. To remove the app's local data from the device, use iOS “Delete App”, rather than “Offload App”. Device backups and shared copies must be managed separately.

Local diagnostic logs contain technical failure information and counts, rather than family names, prompt text, or recording contents. The local log is replaced when it exceeds approximately 256 KB. It is not automatically sent to support.

Contacting support and visiting these pages

If you email support, the developer and the email provider receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request. Send only the details needed; do not send a person's private recordings or photographs unless you intentionally wish to share them for that request. Contact support about questions or deletion of information you have sent.

These pages contain no account, forms, ads, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. When served through GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses and request details, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement. That web hosting is separate from the app's local memory library.
