A little help

Set up Keepsong together and try a short, welcome conversation before a longer story.

Start with familiar details

Finish the welcome flow, then add names, relationships, and optional photographs in People. In Settings, choose spoken prompts, edit “Story prompts”, and add a daily note or routines. You can finish setup and browse without approving recording or enabling the microphone.

If a story will not start

  1. Open the app's Settings tab. Enable “Recording is welcome” only when recording is understood and welcome with appropriate support.
  2. Choose “Enable microphone”. If access was declined, open your device's Settings → Keepsong and enable Microphone, then return to Keepsong.
  3. If the app reports a recording or recovery problem, keep existing files in place, close and reopen Keepsong, and follow its message. Contact support if the problem remains.

Do not delete and reinstall the app to troubleshoot a library or recovery error: deleting it can remove your local memories. Give the device enough free storage and keep the app open until saved playback appears.

Listen, name, and keep a separate copy

Open Memories, select a recording, and choose “Listen to memory”. You can save a title, mark a favorite, or use Share to send a copy to a destination such as Files. Search matches memory titles. Keepsong does not provide its own cloud archive or automatic device transfer.

To remove a recording, choose “Delete this memory” and confirm. This removes its local audio and details after successful deletion; copies shared elsewhere and device backups are separate. If deletion fails, follow the error message and retry after reopening the app.

Return from simple mode

To make the next launch start on the calm check-in, turn on “Open in simple mode” in the app's Settings tab. To return to the family dashboard, open your device's Settings → Keepsong, enable “Open family dashboard”, then close and reopen Keepsong. To enter simple mode again, switch “Open in simple mode” off and back on in the app, then reopen it. Turn that setting off if you no longer want a dedicated check-in device.

Photos and daily cues

A photo held only in iCloud Photos may need an Apple network connection before it can be selected. Try another photo or retry after it is available on your device. Hiding a person keeps their details in the family list. “Use initials instead” removes the displayed photo from that person.

The daily note remains until you change it. Routines are gentle on-screen cues, without notifications or medication tracking. Stop a session whenever the person prefers; choose “Something else” for another invitation or “Not now” to leave the prompt.
