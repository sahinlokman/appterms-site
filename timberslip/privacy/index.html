Privacy Policy

This policy covers TimberSlip version 1.0 for iPhone and iPad. TimberSlip has no account, no developer-operated server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Projects you create: names, notes, boards, sizes, quantities, species, prices, labels and the currency of each project.
  • Your saved species price list (Pro).
  • Unsaved drafts in Quick Calculate and Add Board, so they survive closing the app.
  • Settings such as default currency, length unit, allowance and decimal places.

This information is stored in the app's local storage on the device. TimberSlip does not send it to the developer or to any third party. The calculator works fully offline.

Purchases

TimberSlip Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account and shows the price Apple provides. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Exports and sharing

PDF and CSV files are created on the device only when you choose Export. The system share sheet then sends a copy to the destination you pick, such as Mail, Files or Messages. That destination handles the copy under its own rules. Deleting a project in TimberSlip does not delete copies you have shared.

Backups and deletion

iOS device backups (iCloud or computer backups) may include app data according to your device settings. TimberSlip itself does not provide cloud sync in this version. You can delete a board or project in the app. To remove all app data from the device, delete the app with “Delete App” (not “Offload App”).

Children

TimberSlip is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contacting support and visiting these pages

If you email support, the developer and the email providers involved receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. They are used only to answer your request. Ask at any time to have your messages deleted.

These web pages have no forms, ads, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical connection data such as IP addresses to operate and secure its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.
