What the app does

TimberSlip estimates board feet, linear feet and costs from the sizes, quantities and prices you enter, using the formula thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) × quantity ÷ 12. It is an aid for planning and shopping.

Estimates, not invoices

  • Results depend on what you enter. Check sizes, units and prices before you buy or cut.
  • Billing dimensions and billing board feet reflect the size a seller charges for; they are not physical volume.
  • TimberSlip is not an NHLA grading, surface-measure or log-scaling calculator. Suppliers may measure, round and bill differently, and their invoice is what you pay.
  • An extra purchase allowance is a planning margin, not a guarantee that you will have enough material.
  • Changing the currency changes the symbol only. TimberSlip does not convert currencies or provide market prices.
