How are board feet calculated?

Board feet = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) × quantity ÷ 12. With the length in inches, divide by 144. Metric sizes are converted exactly (1 in = 25.4 mm). Tap Show the math under a result to see every step.

How do I type fractions and feet-inches?

Use the keypad: 3/4, 1 1/4, 1-1/4, .75, 8' 6", 102 in and 25.4mm all work. Fraction keys after a number make a mixed number. Half-finished input is never counted as zero; the field shows what to fix.

Actual vs billing dimensions

Actual dimensions are what you measure. Billing dimensions are what the seller charges for, such as 4/4 (1 in) for a board planed to ¾ in. Empty billing fields use the actual size. Nominal sizes like 2×4 fill actual 1½ × 3½ in and billing 2 × 4 in so both are visible.

Tally vs Estimate

Tally adds up boards exactly as entered. Estimate multiplies your net need by (1 + allowance ÷ 100). A 10% extra purchase allowance covers losing about 9.1% of what you buy; to cover a 10% loss, use 11.11%.

Why doesn't my total match the yard's invoice?

Suppliers may bill by rough or nominal size, round each board, or use NHLA surface measure. Enter the billing size they use, and compare per-line results. Each line cost is rounded half-up to the cent and the total is the sum of line costs.

Restoring TimberSlip Pro

Open Settings & Help and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used to buy Pro. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data?

Everything is stored on your device. There is no account or cloud sync in this version. See the Privacy Policy.
