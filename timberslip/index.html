TimberSlip for iPhone and iPad

Board feet, lumber tally and cost.

TimberSlip helps woodworkers enter fractional lumber sizes quickly, see board feet with the math shown, and total a shopping list with quantity, price and an optional extra purchase allowance. Lists are stored on your device.

A calculator, not an invoice

TimberSlip uses the simple board-foot formula. It is not an NHLA grading, surface-measure or log-scaling tool, and a seller's invoice may differ. Check important orders with your supplier.
