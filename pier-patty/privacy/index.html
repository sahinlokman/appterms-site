Pier Patty

This policy applies only to Pier Patty, package/bundle com.lokman.pierpatty. Contact the developer at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Local play

Game progress (days, cash in game dollars, decorations, quests, keys and chests) and settings (language, sound, vibration) are saved on the device only. There is no account, sign-in, chat or social feature. The game does not ask for a name, date of birth, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included.

Audience and advertising identifiers

Pier Patty is a general-audience game for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and does not ask for an age. Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower. On Android, Google may use the device advertising ID to show, measure and protect ads, and to personalize them where your consent choices allow; you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. On iOS, Apple's tracking prompt is never shown, so the game cannot read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and ads are not based on tracking across other companies' apps. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child's information was provided to us can contact the email above to request deletion.

Optional rewarded ads

From day 3, the closing ledger may offer “Watch an ad · +1 key”. Watching is always optional; skipping it changes nothing else. At most one such reward per closing and three per day are offered. No banner or app-open ads are used, and this version shows no ads between days. Ads are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google's User Messaging Platform checks on every launch whether consent is needed in your region and, if so, asks before any ad is requested. Ad privacy choices can be reopened from Settings → Privacy when Google requires it, and an inappropriate ad can be reported from Settings → Privacy → Report an ad or by email.

To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may process the IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The first seven days are free. The one-time “Full Shop” purchase (pierpatty.fullshop) unlocks the rest of the story. Apple or Google handles payment; we never receive card details. RevenueCat verifies the purchase and restores it, using an anonymous app user identifier, transaction and product information, purchase history, store country, locale and currency. Automatic device identifier collection in RevenueCat is disabled. The unlock is remembered on the device so an owner can keep playing offline. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Sharing, retention and requests

Data is shared only with the providers above for advertising and purchase verification; it is not sold. Local progress stays until you delete the app or its data. Provider transaction records may be retained for legal, accounting and fraud-prevention purposes. To ask about, correct or delete data handled by the developer, email the address above with the app name (and, for purchases, the store order reference). Do not send passwords or full payment details. Requests about Apple, Google or RevenueCat records may also need to go to that provider.

This website measurement is separate from any data processing described above for the app.
