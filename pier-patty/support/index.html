Contact

Email lokman782782@gmail.com with the app name, your device and, for purchases, the store order reference. Never send passwords or full payment details.

Restore the Full Shop

Open Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account used to buy. If the store is unreachable, connect to the internet and try again; an owner who already unlocked the Full Shop keeps playing offline.

Pending or failed payments

If the store says the payment is pending (for example, waiting for a family organizer's approval), the Full Shop opens by itself once it is approved. A cancelled purchase leaves your progress untouched.

Ads and privacy

Rewarded ads are optional. If no ad is available, try later; the closing ledger keeps working. Ad privacy choices are under Settings → Privacy when Google requires them. To report an inappropriate ad use Settings → Privacy → Report an ad.

Delete your data

Deleting the app removes local progress. For provider records, email us; see the privacy policy.
