Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

HomePrivacyTermsSupport

The short version. Skate Flick has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device. We don't run servers that receive your data and the game has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Two partners handle data for us: Google AdMob shows ads to free players, and RevenueCat checks your purchases so they unlock and can be restored.

Skate Flick ("the game") is made by Lokman Sahin, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game and its partners use, why, and the choices you have. Questions: lokman782782@gmail.com.

Data kept on your device

The game saves its state locally on your device: best scores and lines, gaps you've found, your trick book, chosen deck and settings, park goals, collected S-K-A-T-E letters, XP and level, daily challenge progress and streaks, how many sessions you've played, and timers that space out ads and offers. Purchase access is checked through RevenueCat as described below. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it. Purchases can be restored after a reinstall (see below).

Ads (Google AdMob)

Free players see two kinds of ads, served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK: a rewarded ad that you choose to watch to try a locked park for 20 minutes, and an occasional full-screen ad when you return to the menu after a ride. We cap how often ads appear. Buying any park, the park bundle or Skate Flick Pro turns off the full-screen ads, and Pro members see no ads. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (PG).

To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; the Android advertising ID) and the app set ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions (views, taps, rewards) and diagnostic and performance data.

Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and improve its services. Ads are personalised only with your consent. Otherwise Google serves non-personalised or limited ads, which still use the data above for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • Consent message. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, the game shows Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads. You can change your answer any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices, shown wherever it applies.
  • iPhone. The game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the advertising ID is not shared. Change it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android. Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email or card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases, keep track of what you've unlocked and restore it on a new install or device. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random app user ID created by the SDK (a pseudonymous identifier; the game does not ask for your name or email);
  • your purchase history: products, prices, currency, dates, subscription status and the store's transaction and receipt data;
  • basic device and app information, such as operating system, app version, language and store country, plus the IP address of the request.

This is used only to provide what you bought, handle renewals and refunds reported by the store, and keep our sales records. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

The game engine (Unity)

Skate Flick is built with Unity. The Unity engine may send basic device statistics, such as device model, operating system and graphics hardware, to Unity Technologies to help it support devices. It does not include your name or contact details. See Unity's player privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in, and no access to your contacts, photo library, camera, microphone or precise location. Replay photo sharing creates an image locally and opens the operating system share sheet; you choose whether and where to send it.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own. We see only the aggregate reports that Apple, Google, AdMob and RevenueCat provide (installs, crashes, revenue).
  • We don't sell your personal information for money.

Replay photo sharing

When you tap Share in a replay, the game creates a temporary image on your device and passes it to the operating system share sheet. We do not receive that image. If you choose another app or service, its own privacy policy applies to the image you send. The game does not need access to your photo library to show this share sheet.

Children

Skate Flick is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Personalised advertising can count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law; you can opt out through the consent message, Settings > Privacy choices or your device's ad settings.

Because we hold almost no data ourselves, email us at lokman782782@gmail.com and we'll handle your request with RevenueCat, for example deleting the purchase record linked to your app user ID. A store order number helps us find it. For Google's ad data, use My Ad Center or the device settings above. We answer within 30 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as a device model, a problem description or a store order number. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve purchase or technical problems. Please do not send passwords, full payment card details or other unnecessary sensitive information. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations.

Retention

  • Data on your device: until you delete the game.
  • RevenueCat purchase records: for as long as needed to provide your purchases and meet tax and accounting obligations, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Google ad data: according to Google's own retention policies.

International transfers and security

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries. They use safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Data sent between the game and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's update notes.
