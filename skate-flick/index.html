The game

Skate Flick puts a skateboard under your fingertips. Swipe to push and steer, flick to ollie, kickflip and shuvit, and lock into rails and ledges. Every trick comes from the physics, not from a button.

  • Six parks, from a quarry bowl to a rooftop. Two are free to skate.
  • Find every gap in a park and fill your trick book.
  • Chain tricks into lines and chase your best session.
  • No account and no sign-in.
