Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. The Shop tab and the Pro screen have a Restore button too. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Purchases don't move between iPhone and Android.

I paid but the park is still locked.

Check your connection and tap Restore purchases. If the purchase says "Waiting for approval" (for example Ask to Buy or a pending payment), it unlocks as soon as it's approved. Still stuck? Email us your order number.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds go through them: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or Google Play's refund page.

Skate Flick Pro

What do I get?

All six parks, four exclusive Pro decks and no ads, for as long as your subscription is active. Choose monthly or yearly; the yearly plan may start with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel?

  • iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Skate Flick Pro > Cancel Subscription, or Settings > Manage subscription in the game.
  • Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Skate Flick Pro > Cancel.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date (or before the free trial ends) to avoid the next charge. Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription. After cancelling, Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Parks, tries and ads

What is a park try?

On a locked park, tap Watch an ad to ride it for 20 minutes. You'll get a heads-up 2 minutes before the end. When time's up, the try finishes at your next stop, never in the middle of a line. There's a daily limit on tries. If the button says "Ad loading…", wait a moment or check your connection.

How do I turn off ads?

Any purchase (one park, the park bundle or Pro) turns off the full-screen ads between rides. Pro removes ads completely. Free players never see ads during a ride.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where it applies (for example in the EU and UK), open Settings > Privacy choices in the game. On iPhone you can also change tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

Progress

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Scores, found gaps, your trick book, park goals, XP, level and daily challenge progress are saved only on your device, so deleting the game removes them. Purchases come back with Restore purchases.
