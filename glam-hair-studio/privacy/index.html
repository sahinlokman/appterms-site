Glam Hair Studio

This policy applies only to Glam Hair Studio, package/bundle com.starlightstudio.glamhairstudio. Contact the developer at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Local play and age groups

Game progress, settings, earned coins/gems and a coarse age range are stored on the device. We do not request a full date of birth, account registration, real name, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location. Game makeover images are created from game artwork, not photographs of players. Sharing is voluntary and uses the device's share sheet.

Children and mixed audiences

Players under 16 and players who have not selected an age range receive no advertising requests from the game. The age choice stays on the device. Purchases and external links for players under 18 require access through a grown-ups area. The store's own purchase authorization still applies. Core styling gameplay remains free.

Advertising for eligible older players

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform. Where applicable, consent is collected before advertising requests. Google may process IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device identifiers when available, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Advertising requests are limited to G-rated inventory; advertising treatment for teenagers is configured separately. Ad privacy options can be opened from game settings when Google requires them. No banner or app-open ads are used.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google ad controls. Apple tracking permission is not required to play; denying it does not remove access to the game.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment details. RevenueCat validates purchases and restores permanent entitlements, using an anonymous app user identifier, transaction and product information, purchase history, locale and currency. Automatic device identifier collection in RevenueCat is disabled. We do not receive full card details. See RevenueCat privacy information. Purchases require a store connection; earned progress is stored locally. Consumable currency is not a cross-device wallet, and restoration of permanent purchases does not replay consumable bonuses.

Sharing, retention and requests

Data necessary for advertising, purchase verification and fraud prevention is processed by the providers described above; we do not add an independent analytics, social login or advertising mediation SDK. Local progress is retained until app data is cleared or the app is removed. Provider transaction records may be retained for legal, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations. To request information, correction or deletion of data handled by the developer, contact the email above. Include the app name and store order reference if relevant; do not send passwords or full payment details. Requests concerning Apple, Google or another sharing destination may also need to be directed to that provider.
