Glam Hair Studio

This policy applies only to Glam Hair Studio, package/bundle com.starlightstudio.glamhairstudio. Contact the developer at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Local play

Game progress, settings and earned coins/gems are stored on the device. We do not request a full date of birth, account registration, real name, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location. Game makeover images are created from game artwork, not photographs of players. Sharing is voluntary and uses the device's share sheet.

Audience

Glam Salon is a general-audience casual game. It is not directed to children and has no age screen or account. Purchases are authorised by Apple or Google with the account holder's own credentials and family controls (such as Ask to Buy). Core styling gameplay remains free.

Children under 13

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child's information was provided to us can contact the email above to request deletion.

Reminders and ratings

Players may be asked once for permission to receive local reminders (for example, a daily gift). These notifications are scheduled on the device by the game itself; no push service, token or server is used, and permission can be changed at any time in the device settings. The game may once show Apple's standard rating prompt; Apple controls that prompt and any rating you leave.

Advertising

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform. On iOS the system tracking (ATT) prompt is shown first; if you decline, no further consent prompt is shown and ads are not personalized. Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown after you allow tracking, before personalized advertising requests. Google may process IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device identifiers when available, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Advertising requests are limited to ads rated up to PG (suitable for ages 9 and up, matching the game’s 9+ rating). You can report an inappropriate ad from Settings → Privacy and support → Report an ad, or by emailing the address above. Ad privacy options can be opened from game settings when Google requires them. No banner or app-open ads are used.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google ad controls. Apple tracking permission is not required to play; denying it does not remove access to the game.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment details. RevenueCat validates purchases and restores permanent entitlements, using an anonymous app user identifier, transaction and product information, purchase history, locale and currency. Automatic device identifier collection in RevenueCat is disabled. We do not receive full card details. See RevenueCat privacy information. Purchases require a store connection; earned progress is stored locally. Consumable currency is not a cross-device wallet, and restoration of permanent purchases does not replay consumable bonuses.

Sharing, retention and requests

Data necessary for advertising, purchase verification and fraud prevention is processed by the providers described above; we do not add an independent analytics, social login or advertising mediation SDK. Local progress is retained until app data is cleared or the app is removed. Provider transaction records may be retained for legal, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations. To request information, correction or deletion of data handled by the developer, contact the email above. Include the app name and store order reference if relevant; do not send passwords or full payment details. Requests concerning Apple, Google or another sharing destination may also need to be directed to that provider.
