BabyLook Privacy Policy

Updated November 8, 2026. This document covers BabyLook only.

Contact: lokman782782@gmail.com

Photos and AI processing

When you create a portrait, BabyLook sends the two reference photos you selected (which may contain face data) and the portrait request through its Oracle-hosted backend in Frankfurt to Snapgen, our image-generation processor, using the Nano Banana 2 model. Growing-up portraits use the revealed child portrait as the reference. Nothing is uploaded until you give explicit consent in the app. The result is an artistic illustration, not a genetic or medical prediction.

Photos are transmitted to Snapgen solely to generate the image you requested. BabyLook does not use your photos or generated images to train AI models. BabyLook's backend passes the photos through for the duration of the request and does not store them on its servers afterwards; BabyLook application logs do not include your photos. The provider returns the generated image as a URL, which the app downloads and keeps on your device. Snapgen's own handling and retention of data it receives is described in Snapgen's privacy policy. Revoking consent in Settings stops future uploads; it does not by itself remove data already received by the provider.

Third parties and equal protection

We share personal data only with the service providers named in this policy (Snapgen for image generation, Oracle Cloud for hosting, RevenueCat and Apple App Store or Google Play for purchases), only for the purpose described here, and not for advertising. BabyLook shares the data with these providers only so they can provide their service to BabyLook, and requires the same or equal protection of that data as described in this policy; each provider's handling is also governed by its own published terms and privacy policy. If we learn that a provider does not protect the data to that level, we will stop sending it data.

Local data

Chosen photos are kept in app memory for the current flow. Generated portraits, keepsakes, settings and usage information are stored on your device. You may delete photographs/history and revoke processing consent in Settings. Keepsake names and notes are rendered locally and are not sent to the AI provider. Saving or sharing exports the selected image to the destination you choose.

Baby Journal

The Baby Journal is optional. Your baby's name, birth or due date, gender choice, memories, journal photos and reminder time are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Journal data is not uploaded to BabyLook's backend, is not sent to the AI provider, and is not synchronized through iCloud, CloudKit or any other cloud service by BabyLook. Like other app data, it may be included in device backups you make yourself (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup), under your own backup settings. Journal reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no push-notification service is used. You can delete the whole journal (baby profile, memories, photos and reminders) with Delete Journal in the journal profile, delete individual memories, or delete the app. Saving or sharing a memory exports it to the destination you choose.

Reporting content

If you use the in-app Report option on a generated image, the image is removed from your device and BabyLook's server stores the report for safety review: the image's task identifier, the reason you chose, any optional note you write (up to 500 characters), the app version and platform, the time of the report, and the account identifier the app already uses for usage limits (a hashed identifier on iOS, the anonymous installation ID on Android). The reported image itself and your photos are not uploaded with the report. Open reports are kept until they are reviewed; reviewed reports are deleted after one year. Reports are not used for advertising or AI training.

Usage and subscriptions

RevenueCat and Apple App Store or Google Play process installation identifiers, purchase and subscription information to provide billing and restore. The backend verifies subscriptions before paid generation. On iOS, Apple-signed AppTransaction and transaction proofs establish access; raw account identifiers are hashed for quota records. On Android, Play Integrity evaluates app/device/licensing integrity. An anonymous server-generated installation ID and secure session establish access; the backend uses RevenueCat to verify Google Play entitlement. Usage markers are kept without an automatic expiry to enforce one free portrait; paid generation timestamps use a rolling seven-day quota. Operational logs may contain IP/network metadata and request/error timestamps; BabyLook application logs do not include selected photos or session secrets.

No login or cloud photo synchronization

This version does not provide a login, a shared account or history across iOS and Android. Android free allowance is installation-based and may reset on reinstall. No advertising SDK is integrated in this application.

Your choices

Use only photos you have permission to process. Do not upload someone else's photos without consent. Contact us for privacy questions or requests, including requests about reports you have submitted. Requests affecting data held by a provider are handled within that provider's capabilities; deleting local history does not delete data held remotely.
