BabyLook Terms of Use

Updated November 8, 2026. These terms cover BabyLook only.

The service

BabyLook creates entertainment-oriented AI portraits. Images are artistic illustrations: they do not predict actual appearance, genetics or health. Results may be inaccurate or vary between requests. Portraits are made from fixed templates for the themes, ages and options shown in the app; you cannot enter your own prompts. Use only images you are authorized to provide and obtain permission from the people represented.

Free use and Premium

One standard, watermarked classic portrait (age 1, with a randomly chosen gender) is available without a subscription. On iOS the server applies a lifetime account allowance. Android uses an installation-based allowance; reinstall persistence is not guaranteed. Without Premium, the Baby Journal keeps up to three memories and the standard set of editing tools.

BabyLook Premium (BabyLook Pro) is offered as two auto-renewing subscriptions: weekly at US$0.99 per week and annual at US$24.99 per year (United States prices; local prices, currencies and taxes may vary by App Store or Google Play storefront). Premium includes up to five generated images in any rolling seven-day period, all portrait themes (Cozy knits, Birthday, Garden and Storybook), growing-up portraits at ages 3, 5 and 10, gender choice, watermark-free HD images and full Baby Journal access: unlimited memories and all premium stickers, layouts, backgrounds, fonts and filters. One generated image uses one credit; collections use one credit per image, so a four-portrait collection uses four credits. Discontinued plans that were previously purchased keep their Premium access for the verified paid period. Local keepsake exports do not use generation credits. No subscription free trial is offered.

Billing and auto-renewal

The price, billing period and renewal terms that apply to you are displayed by your store before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple ID (or Google Play account) when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends. The annual plan is paid upfront for one year. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings (or Google Play subscriptions); cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period. Apple or Google handles payment, cancellation and applicable refund processes. Pending or unverified payments do not unlock paid generation. Subscriptions are not shared between iOS and Android in this version.

Reporting content

In the iOS app, open a generated portrait on the result screen or in History, tap Report, choose a reason and optionally add a note. The reported image is removed from your device right away, and the report is sent to BabyLook for safety review. On Android, and on any platform, you can also report content or abuse by email to lokman782782@gmail.com. We aim to review reports within 24 hours. If a report is confirmed, we may block the responsible account from further generation and suspend or terminate its access; images held by our AI provider, Snapgen, are handled through Snapgen. BabyLook does not keep copies of generated images on its own servers.
