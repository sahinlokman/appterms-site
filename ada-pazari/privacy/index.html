Privacy Policy

This policy explains data handling in Ada Pazarı, the iPhone and iPad game provided by Lokman Şahin. It applies to this game and does not describe other apps.

Game information stored on your device

Money, goods, crops and workshops, shelves, workers, customers, orders, progress on both islands, guild contracts, research, dates of earned daily rewards and tutorial steps are stored on your device. Language, sound, music, ambience and vibration preferences are local too. Save and last-played timestamps support offline progress and help prevent duplicate rewards. Local backup and recovery files help protect your progress.

During normal gameplay these saves are not sent to a developer-operated server. The game has no account, automatic cloud sync or online multiplayer system.

Exporting and importing progress

When you choose Export in Settings, your game progress is converted to a transfer code and copied to the system clipboard. Import only processes a code that you paste into the field and confirm; the game does not read your clipboard in the background. The code is not an encrypted vault and contains your game save. Keep it somewhere you trust. If you share it through another app or service, that service's data practices apply. The developer does not automatically receive your code.

Advertising, analytics and permissions

This version has no advertising, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Third-party advertising, tracking and analytics services are not enabled. The game does not request an advertising identifier or access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location. Music and ambience are generated inside the app; no audio is recorded.

Information you send for support

If you choose to email lokman782782@gmail.com, your email address, message and attachments you select are processed to answer your request and investigate the issue. Do not send unnecessary personal information, passwords or payment details. You can contact the same address to request access to or deletion of support correspondence. Your email provider's own privacy practices also apply.

Deletion and backups

Deleting the app removes its local app data; operating-system backups may remain separately. You control transfer codes you save and copies shared through other services. You can replace a clipboard code by copying different text. The developer cannot remotely delete local saves or personal backups that the developer does not hold.

This website and external links

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. No account, form, advertising, analytics script or external font has been added to them. The hosting service may process technical access information, such as an IP address and request time, to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening these pages from the game makes a web request through your browser.

If game data handling or services change, this policy and the related store information will be updated. Privacy questions and requests: lokman782782@gmail.com.
