Privacy Policy

Updated: October 24, 2026 · Toy Plane: Airplane Games 1.0

About this release

Toy Plane: Airplane Games 1.0 does not require a player account. Game progress is stored on your device. Optional purchases use Apple App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android, together with RevenueCat; optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob. Game Center and in-game iCloud synchronization are disabled.

Developer and contact

Toy Plane: Airplane Games is provided by independent developer Lokman Şahin. Contact us about privacy or support at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game stores coins, planes, paints, upgrades, records, missions, daily rewards, discovery progress and sound, language, control and graphics preferences. Two rotating local save files help recover interrupted writes. Personal ghost recordings and temporary result images are also stored locally. These copies are in the app's own data area and are not an external backup service.

Local diagnostics

A limited local diagnostic log records flights, rewards, frame-rate measurements, errors, application version and device model. These files are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. Unity Analytics and Unity cloud crash reporting are disabled in this release. The game uses the Unity engine; see the Unity Privacy Policy.

Ads and consent

Google AdMob supplies optional rewarded ads. On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before opening Google's consent form or starting the advertising SDK. If you allow tracking, Google and its partners may link data from this app with data from other companies' apps or websites for personalized advertising and measurement, subject to your separate consent choices where required. If you deny or restrict tracking, the game disables ad personalization and its publisher first-party identifier and requests limited ads without local advertising identifiers. Google and its partners may still process IP addresses (including approximate location), ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and manage consent. No precise location permission is requested. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings and revisit available Google consent choices through Settings → Ad privacy. Declining permission does not prevent basic gameplay. See Google Privacy Policy.

On Android, Google UMP checks your consent choices before the advertising SDK starts or requests ads. Depending on your choices and region, Google may process the Android advertising ID, app set ID or other device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic/performance data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The publisher first-party ID is disabled. Where available, use Settings → Ad privacy to manage your choices; Android advertising ID controls are available in device settings. No contacts, photographs, microphone or precise location permissions are requested.

Purchase processing

Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device, processes payment; we do not receive your card details. RevenueCat processes purchase history, receipts or transaction identifiers, entitlement status, an app-generated anonymous user identifier, and technical information needed to validate and restore purchases, prevent fraud and analyze purchase activity. This does not synchronize your game progress. Records may be retained for purchase restoration, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Contact support for access or deletion requests; Store billing records are managed by Apple or Google Play. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Reminders and sharing

If you opt in, reminders are scheduled locally after notification permission. No remote push server is used. Disable reminders in the game or device settings. If you share a result, the game creates a temporary screenshot and opens the system share sheet; only the destination you choose receives the content you share, under its own terms. External privacy and support links open in your browser.

Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and chosen attachments. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot, retaining them only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, without advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies added by us. The hosting provider may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local progress remains until the app's data is removed; uninstalling may remove it. Device backups can remain according to operating-system settings. This release has no cross-device progress recovery. Temporary files can be cleared by the operating system. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests concerning information you sent us, email lokman782782@gmail.com. Rights under applicable privacy law, including KVKK and GDPR where applicable, are not limited by this policy. We do not directly sell information you send to support. Ad-related processing and choices are described above.

Children and changes

The game does not ask players for names, email addresses, contacts, photographs or precise location. If a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Changes to data practices will be reflected here with a revised date. This policy covers Toy Plane: Airplane Games only, not other games on appterms.site.
