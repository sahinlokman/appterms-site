Privacy Policy

Updated: October 16, 2026 · Real Plane Surf 0.4.0

About this release

Real Plane Surf 0.4.0 does not require an account. Progress and preferences are stored on your device. This release has no active real-money purchases, third-party advertising, Game Center sign-in or in-game iCloud synchronization.

Developer and contact

Real Plane Surf is provided by independent developer Lokman Şahin. Contact us about privacy or support at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game stores coins, planes, paints, upgrades, records, missions, daily rewards, discovery progress and sound, language, control and graphics preferences. Two rotating local save files help recover interrupted writes. Personal ghost recordings and temporary result images are also stored locally. These copies are in the app's own data area and are not an external backup service.

Local diagnostics and Unity packages

A limited local diagnostic log records flights, upgrades, rewards, frame-rate measurements, errors, application version and device model. This release does not upload those files to a developer-operated server. The game uses the Unity engine and includes Unity Analytics and purchasing packages. Version 0.4.0 is not linked to a Unity Cloud project, so the game does not start cloud analytics. “Share anonymous data” is off by default; enabling the preference in this release does not configure a cloud destination. Before future cloud collection is enabled, this policy will be updated and consent requested where required. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Advertising, purchases and Apple services

There is no active advertising network, advertising identifier (IDFA) request or tracking across other companies' apps and websites. Simulated ads are limited to development builds. Real-money purchases, restoration, Game Center and game-managed iCloud saves are disabled. Earned coins have no cash value. Device backups, App Store downloads and operating-system diagnostics may be handled by Apple according to your device settings and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Reminders and sharing

If you opt in, reminders are scheduled locally after notification permission. No remote push server is used. Disable reminders in the game or device settings. If you share a result, the game creates a temporary screenshot and opens the system share sheet; only the destination you choose receives the content you share, under its own terms. External privacy and support links open in your browser.

Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and chosen attachments. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot, retaining them only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, without advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies added by us. The hosting provider may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local progress remains until the app's data is removed; uninstalling may remove it. Device backups can remain according to operating-system settings. This release has no cross-device progress recovery. Temporary files can be cleared by the operating system. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests concerning information you sent us, email lokman782782@gmail.com. Rights under applicable privacy law, including KVKK and GDPR where applicable, are not limited by this policy. We do not sell personal information.

Children and changes

The game does not ask players for names, email addresses, contacts, photographs or precise location. If a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Changes to data practices will be reflected here with a revised date. This policy covers Real Plane Surf only, not other games on appterms.site.
