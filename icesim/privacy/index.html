Privacy Policy

Updated: October 21, 2026 · IceSim: Ice Cream Cooking Game 2.0

About this release

IceSim does not require a player account. Game progress is stored on your device. The optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase uses Apple and RevenueCat; ads are provided by Google AdMob. Game Center, iCloud synchronization, notifications, the camera and the photo library are not used.

Developer and contact

IceSim is provided by independent developer Lokman Şahin. Contact us about privacy or support at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game stores your level, coins, tips upgrade level, owned and selected cones, mystery cone progress, perfect-star streak, the ingredients collected in your pantry, the day you last spun the daily wheel, and your sound and vibration preferences. This information is kept in the app's own data area and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Diagnostics

IceSim does not include its own analytics or crash reporting tools. Google's advertising SDK may collect diagnostic and performance data as described below. If you share crash reports with developers through iOS settings, Apple provides them under its own policy.

Ads, consent and tracking

The free version shows Google AdMob full-screen ads when you return to the home screen after a level, starting after the first two levels and at most once every 30 seconds. Google and its partners may process IP addresses (including approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and other purposes described in the consent screen. Google UMP presents consent choices where required; ads are requested only when it allows. Where shown, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt decides whether the advertising identifier (IDFA) is available to Google. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and, where required, revisit ad choices through Settings → Privacy Options in the game. Rejecting consent or tracking does not prevent gameplay. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchase processing

Apple processes payment; we do not receive your card details. RevenueCat processes purchase history, receipts or transaction identifiers, purchase status, an app-generated anonymous user identifier, and technical information needed to validate and restore purchases, prevent fraud and analyze purchase activity. It does not synchronize your game progress. Records may be retained for purchase restoration, fraud prevention and legal obligations. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and chosen attachments. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot, retaining them only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, without advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies added by us. The hosting provider may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local progress remains until the app's data is removed; uninstalling the game deletes it. Device backups can remain according to operating-system settings. Google, RevenueCat and Apple retain service data under their own policies. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests concerning information you sent us, email lokman782782@gmail.com. Rights under applicable privacy law, including KVKK and GDPR where applicable, are not limited by this policy. We do not sell information you send to support.

Children and changes

IceSim is a general-audience game. It does not ask players for names, email addresses, contacts or precise location. Parents should supervise children using ad-supported features or purchases. If a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Changes to data practices will be reflected here with a revised date. This policy covers IceSim only, not other games on appterms.site.
