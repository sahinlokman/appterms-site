Privacy Policy

Updated: November 4, 2026 · IceSim: Ice Cream Cooking Game 2.0

About this release

IceSim does not require a player account. Game progress is stored on your device. The optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase uses Apple on iOS or Google Play on Android, together with RevenueCat; ads are provided by Google AdMob. An optional daily reminder notification is scheduled on the device itself after you allow it; nothing is sent to it from a server, and you can turn it off in the device settings. Game Center, iCloud synchronization, the camera and the photo library are not used.

Developer and contact

IceSim is provided by independent developer Lokman Şahin. Contact us about privacy or support at lokman782782@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game stores your level, coins, tips upgrade level, owned and selected cones, mystery cone progress, perfect-star streak, the ingredients collected in your pantry, daily wheel, daily gift, daily challenge and goal progress, winter event progress, the tip jar timer, how many optional reward videos were used today, your notification choice, on iOS the age you enter at the first start (kept as an approximate birth year), and your sound and vibration preferences. This information is kept in the app's own data area and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Diagnostics and game settings

IceSim does not include its own analytics or crash reporting tools. When it starts, the game downloads a small settings file from appterms.site that adjusts ad timing and reward amounts; the request contains no player or game data, though the hosting provider receives the IP address as with any web request. Google's advertising SDK may collect diagnostic and performance data as described below. Apple or Google may provide platform diagnostics under their own policies and your device settings.

Ads and consent

The free version can show a Google AdMob full-screen ad when you return to the home screen after a level. None are shown in your first play session, at most one every 75 seconds and at most eight per session, never right after a reward video and never after a one-star level. Reward videos are always optional: they are offered on buttons marked ▶ (for example double tips, a second chance, extra coins or 30 minutes without ads) and the game can always be played without them. Google UMP presents consent choices where required; ads are requested only when it allows. Where required, you can revisit ad choices through Settings → Privacy Options in the game. On iOS the game asks once, at the first start, how old you are; the answer stays on your device and only decides how ads are requested. Players who are under 14 by that year are never asked for tracking. Older players may see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if they allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is available to Google and ads may be personalized. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads stay limited to a G content rating for everyone. Rejecting consent or tracking does not prevent gameplay. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Family-safe advertising

IceSim is intended for all ages, including children. Every Android player, and every iOS player who enters an age under 14, receives family-safe advertising: ad requests receive child-directed treatment, a G maximum ad content rating and disabled personalization and publisher first-party identifiers. On Android the advertising ID and Android ad-services permissions are removed; on iOS these players are never asked for tracking. UMP is informed that players may be under the age of consent. No third-party mediation SDK is included. Google may still process IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, app-instance or service identifiers and diagnostic information to deliver contextual ads, secure the service and prevent fraud. On Android these restrictions apply to adults as well because the Android version does not ask for age.

Purchase processing

Apple on iOS or Google Play on Android processes payment; we do not receive your card details. RevenueCat processes purchase history, receipts or transaction identifiers, purchase status, an app-generated anonymous user identifier, and technical information needed to validate and restore purchases, prevent fraud and analyze purchase activity. It does not synchronize your game progress. Records may be retained for purchase restoration, fraud prevention and legal obligations. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and chosen attachments. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot, retaining them only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, without advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies added by us. The hosting provider may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local progress remains until the app's data is removed; uninstalling the game deletes it. Device backups can remain according to operating-system settings. Google, RevenueCat and Apple retain service data under their own policies. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests concerning information you sent us, email lokman782782@gmail.com. Rights under applicable privacy law, including KVKK and GDPR where applicable, are not limited by this policy. We do not sell information you send to support.

How to request IceSim data deletion

Email lokman782782@gmail.com with the subject "IceSim data deletion". State whether you want your support correspondence or purchase-service record deleted. If you purchased Remove Ads, you may include your store order identifier to help us locate the record; never send card details, passwords or your entire store account history. We may request only the information necessary to verify and locate your record. Once verified, we remove the identifiable support correspondence and the associated RevenueCat customer record where available. Deleting a purchase-service record can affect restoration until the store receipt is submitted again; it does not refund a purchase or delete the store's own transaction record. Local game progress can be deleted immediately using Android Settings → Apps → IceSim → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling. Google advertising-service data and Google/Apple store records are controlled by those providers under their privacy policies. Any records that must be retained for legal obligations remain only for the applicable legally required period; if such an exception applies to your request, we explain the retained categories, reason and period in our response.

Children and changes

IceSim is intended for all ages, including children. It does not ask players for names, email addresses, contacts or precise location. Parents should supervise children using ad-supported features or purchases. If a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Changes to data practices will be reflected here with a revised date. This policy covers IceSim only, not other games on appterms.site.
