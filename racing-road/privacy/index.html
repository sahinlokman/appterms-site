Privacy Policy

Racing Road: Cyber Overdrive · Effective 6 November 2026

This policy applies only to Racing Road: Cyber Overdrive (Android and iOS package com.racingroad.cyberoverdrive). Contact: lokman782782@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

The game stores race progress, car unlocks, settings, virtual currency, daily reward dates and purchase fulfillment records on your device. No first-party account or public profile is required. The game does not offer chat, public user uploads or location-based features. We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Age group choice

At first launch, the app asks for an age group (under 13, 13–17, 18+, or prefer not to say). Only this broad choice is stored locally; no birth date is requested. The choice does not unlock rewards or change prices. The current app uses the same protective, non-personalized advertising settings for every group, including unknown ages.

Family audience and advertising

Children are included in the intended audience. The app applies child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment to every Google Mobile Ads request, with a General audience content rating. It does not request Apple's tracking authorization or use personalized advertising. Automatic interstitial advertisements, banner advertisements and automatic purchase offers are disabled in the family configuration. Optional rewarded advertisements may be offered for a clearly stated in-game bonus. Playing and collecting normal race rewards do not require watching an advertisement.

Google AdMob processes information needed to deliver contextual advertisements, prevent fraud and measure delivery. Depending on the platform and applicable SDK behavior, this can include IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device/app technical information, advertisement interactions and diagnostic information. Child-directed settings restrict interest-based advertising; they do not mean that the advertising SDK processes no data. See Google's partner-site and app data explanation and Google's Privacy Policy. Applicable privacy options are available from the app's parent area. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform handles privacy messages before advertisements can be requested.

Purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment and any store-required parent authentication. We do not receive payment card numbers. Unity In-App Purchasing handles product information, purchase and restore transactions, and fulfillment. Purchase identifiers and entitlement records are used to deliver items and prevent duplicate rewards. Unity IAP can also process purchase, app/device and diagnostic information under its SDK data practices. The app excludes IAP's IDFA integration on iOS. See Unity's game player privacy policy, Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Analytics and tracking

The game does not initialize a separate first-party behavioral analytics, social-login or cross-app tracking service. Local gameplay and monetization counters are used by the app. The advertising, purchase and platform SDKs may process operational information as described above. We do not sell children's personal information or knowingly use it for behavioral advertising.

Parent controls and external links

Purchases and links outside the game are placed behind a parent gate. The gate is an additional interaction barrier; store authentication remains the payment authorization. Parents should enable purchase approvals on their device. No answer entered into the parent gate is sent to us.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until reset or removed. Settings provides a progress reset. Deleting the app removes local data according to operating-system backup behavior. Restorable purchases remain associated with your store account; deleting progress does not cancel a purchase or issue a refund. Advertising and store providers retain their own records according to their policies. If you contact support, we use your email and the details you provide to answer your request and keep them only as needed for support and legal obligations.

Privacy rights and children's information

A parent or guardian can contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information we hold. Please identify the game and provide only information needed for the request. Do not send a child's name, photos, payment card details, passwords or store credentials. We do not ask children to submit personal information through the game. If we learn that personal information was collected from a child contrary to applicable requirements, we will investigate and take appropriate deletion or restriction steps.

Changes

We update this page when the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
