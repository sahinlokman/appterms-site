Email lokman782782@gmail.com. Please include your device model, operating system version, game version and the steps that led to the problem. Parents or guardians should contact support for children.

Missing purchase

Open Settings and choose Restore with the same store account that made the purchase. Permanent content can be restored. Currency already delivered or spent is not refilled by Restore. If a purchase is awaiting parental or store approval, it will be delivered after the store reports approval. Never send your store password, payment card number or a child's personal details.

Advertisement unavailable

Advertisements depend on network and provider availability. Continue playing and claim the normal race reward. Optional extra bonuses require the advertisement provider to confirm completion. An ad-free purchase disables advertisements.

Privacy or data requests

Use the same support address and identify Racing Road: Cyber Overdrive. Settings → parent area contains the privacy policy, terms, support and any required advertising privacy options. Progress reset does not cancel store purchases or issue refunds.
