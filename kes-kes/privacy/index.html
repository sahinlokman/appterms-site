Privacy Policy

Lokman Sahin provides KES KES! Soap Cutting ASMR, package com.soapworld.cutter. This policy explains the Android 2.0 game with advertising and an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase. The iPhone/iPad 2.0 build prepared on October 23, 2026 has a separate offline implementation without these advertising and purchase SDKs. Earlier store versions may differ; contact us with your platform and version if you need help.

iPhone/iPad 2.0.1 advertising and purchases

The iOS 2.0.1 update prepared on October 25, 2026 includes a Google AdMob home-screen banner, limited interstitials at completed campaign-level transitions, and optional rewarded ads on non-Zen result screens. Interstitials protect the first three levels, occur no more often than every three completed campaign transitions and are at least 120 seconds after the previous full-screen ad. Choosing the rewarded ad can grant 30 virtual coins only after the ad network confirms the reward; normal rewards and progression do not require viewing an ad. A verified ad-free entitlement removes all advertising and lets the player claim the same optional result bonus without an ad. iOS 2.0.0 remains the separate offline version described above. The new update uses Google Mobile Ads, UMP and RevenueCat. Ad services may process approximate location derived from IP address, device identifiers, ad and app interactions, crash/performance information and other diagnostics for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. The app requests non-personalized ads for all age groups and does not request Apple's IDFA tracking permission; this does not mean that no data is processed.

The selected age band is stored locally. Unknown-age users may continue playing without ads. We request child-directed treatment through age 12, under-age-of-consent treatment below 18, and a maximum G ad-content rating. Regional consent is checked before ads load. Required ad privacy choices, age changes, purchase controls and external links are available in Settings → Parents & purchases. Non-adult and unknown-age users must complete the parental-area challenge.

RevenueCat uses an anonymous app identifier and Apple purchase/transaction information to validate and restore the existing permanent ad-free entitlement. Automatic device-identifier collection and optional diagnostics are disabled. No name, email, telephone number or advertising identifier is attached to RevenueCat. Apple handles payments and refunds. A new Remove Ads purchase is offered only when the App Store returns an available product and its localized price; store approval or missing metadata can make it unavailable. Playing without purchasing remains possible. Network access is required for validation and restoration; the last entitlement known to the SDK is retained offline, and ads remain off when entitlement status has not been resolved.

Local game data and age group

No developer account is required. Progress, coins, unlocked items, settings and the selected age band are stored on the device. Core gameplay works offline. Android asks for an age band, not a name or date of birth. The age selection determines child/teen advertising treatment; the app does not send your selected age band to a developer database. Platform backups may include local saves. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove local progress.

Android advertising: Google AdMob and consent

Android uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Advertising services may collect and share IP addresses (which can reveal approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device or other identifiers to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep services reliable. This app removes the Android advertising ID permission and disables personalized ad requests for every age group. These choices do not mean that no data is processed.

We request child-directed treatment for users aged 12 or younger, under-age-of-consent treatment for users under 18, and a maximum G ad-content rating for all users. Users with an unknown age do not receive ads. Children are served only through the Google AdMob SDK with child treatment enabled. The app uses no additional ad-network mediation SDK. The game does not ask for precise location, contacts, microphone, camera or photos. Google's consent choices appear where required and available. Privacy choices can be reopened from Settings → Parents & purchases → Ad privacy choices when Google requires that option.

Rewarded ads are optional and normal progression remains available without watching one. Interstitials occur only at natural level transitions, no more often than every three completed levels and at least 120 seconds apart. Removing ads prevents both ad formats; the same bonus can then be obtained without an ad.

Android purchases: Google Play and RevenueCat

The optional permanent Remove Ads product is a non-consumable, not a subscription. Google Play processes payments; we do not receive card details. RevenueCat validates purchase tokens and purchase history and maintains the ad-free entitlement. It receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase/transaction information, along with technical information needed to communicate with its service. We do not attach your name, email, telephone number or advertising identifier to RevenueCat. Automatic device-identifier collection and optional diagnostics are disabled. Purchase data is used for app functionality, entitlement restoration and purchase analytics. Network access is needed to load prices, validate or restore purchases. Restore uses your Google Play account.

Children and parental area

The game is intended for a mixed audience including children. Purchase and external-link controls are in a parental area with a challenge for child or unknown-age users; Google Play may additionally require its own purchase authentication. We do not knowingly request children's names, contact details or other personal information in the game. A parent can contact us about a child's data without sending the child's full name, birth date or other sensitive information.

Retention, deletion and support

Local saves remain until removed through your device controls. Purchase records are retained as needed to provide lifetime access, handle refunds and meet legal/accounting requirements. Providers apply their own retention policies. You may request deletion of support conversations and RevenueCat customer records by emailing lokman782782@gmail.com. To locate an anonymous purchase record, provide the Google Play order reference privately if available; never send a password or card number. Deleting purchase records may affect restoration, and records that must legally be retained cannot always be erased immediately. We will explain any limitation. The game has no developer account to close.

Support emails are processed by your and our email providers so we can respond. Only send information needed for the issue. Our static website has no advertising, account forms or app analytics; GitHub Pages may process technical access logs to operate and protect it. Service traffic uses encrypted connections. Providers may process information outside your country under their privacy arrangements.
