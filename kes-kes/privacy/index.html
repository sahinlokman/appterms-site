Privacy Policy

This policy describes the KES KES! iPhone and iPad game.

Data in the game

Game progress, unlocked toys and knives, earned in-game coins, scores and settings are stored locally on your device. The game does not require an account. The current game build does not send this information to a developer server, use analytics or advertising SDKs, request tracking permission, or offer an in-app purchase. iOS device backups may include locally stored game data according to your device settings.

Support email

If you email us for support, your email provider and ours process your address and message so we can reply. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. You may ask us to delete a support conversation by emailing lokman782782@gmail.com.

This website

These static pages have no account form, app analytics or advertising. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical access records to operate and protect the site.

Changes and contact

If a future game build adds online services or third-party SDKs, we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before that build is released. For privacy questions, email lokman782782@gmail.com.
